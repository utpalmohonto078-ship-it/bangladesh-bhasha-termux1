# Research Scope

## Scientific
Mathematics, statistics, numerical methods, physics simulations,
data analysis and engineering research.

## Space
Public satellite datasets, Earth observation, telemetry analysis
and scientific visualization.

## Intelligence
Lawful OSINT, public government information, public news,
threat intelligence and evidence provenance.

## Cybersecurity
Defensive security, vulnerability analysis, secure development,
authorized testing and incident response.

## Restrictions
No unauthorized access, theft of secrets, destructive activity,
or illicit procurement and distribution of stolen information.
